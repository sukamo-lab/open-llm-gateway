# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in LLM Gateway spaces must be respectful, professional, and constructive. Examples of positive behavior include assuming good intent, discussing technical decisions with evidence, respecting boundaries, accepting correction, and focusing criticism on ideas and implementations rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, and retaliation against good-faith reporters are unacceptable.

This standard applies to repository activity, issue and review discussions, project communication, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement decisions consider severity, context, prior behavior, safety, and the interests of the community. Maintainers are not required to provide repeated warnings.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private reporting form:

https://github.com/sukamo-lab/open-llm-gateway/security/advisories/new

Identify the report as a Code of Conduct matter and include relevant links or screenshots. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. A maintainer named in a report must not participate in deciding that report when another authorized maintainer is available.

Bad-faith or knowingly false reports may themselves violate this policy. Good-faith disagreement, criticism, or an unproven report does not.
